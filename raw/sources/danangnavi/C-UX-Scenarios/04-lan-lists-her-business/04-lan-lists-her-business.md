# 04: Lan Lists Her Business

**Project:** DaNangNavi
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)
**Design Intent:** Dream Up (D)
**Design Status:** not-started

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Create a business listing that reaches Japanese customers — upload photos, get an auto-translated menu, and create her first coupon, all in a Vietnamese interface she can understand.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** #6 Business Onboarding (300 businesses) + #7 Business Satisfaction (70%+)
**Objective:** 300 registered businesses, 70% owner satisfaction within 6 months

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Lan (Tertiary)
**Situation:** 38-year-old Vietnamese restaurant owner near My Khe Beach. A DaNangNavi rep visited last week. She's seen 2-3 Japanese customers mention "DaNangNavi" recently and her competitor down the street just got listed. Quiet Tuesday afternoon, she decides to try it.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Get visible to the Japanese tourists who walk past her restaurant every day but never come in.

**Worry:** Waste time on a complicated platform that won't actually bring customers.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (smartphone)
**Entry:** Clicks the registration link the DaNangNavi rep sent via Zalo, opens on her phone during a slow Tuesday afternoon.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Listing live with translated menu and photos in under 15 minutes, first coupon created — feels "this was easy, let's see if it works."

**Business Success:**
New business onboarded toward 300 target, coupon ready to drive trackable redemptions, Vietnamese-language dashboard reducing support needs.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Listing Editor** — Creates listing: uploads 5 dish photos, fills basic info in Vietnamese (auto-translated to Japanese), uploads menu photos
2. **Coupon Manager** — Creates first coupon "10% off for DaNangNavi users," sets validity period, previews how Japanese customers see it
3. **Business Dashboard** — Views her new dashboard: initial status, learns where to check views/clicks/redemptions later ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Lan the Listing Owner (Tertiary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** Gain direct visibility to Japanese customers who are actively searching for her business type
- ❌ **Fear:** Investing time and money in a platform that doesn't deliver real, countable customers

**Business Goal:** #6 Business Onboarding (300 businesses) + #7 Business Satisfaction (70%+)
