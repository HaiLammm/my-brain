# 04.2 Coupon Manager

**Scenario:** 04: Lan Lists Her Business
**Page Number:** 04.2
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Create a first coupon ("10% off for DaNangNavi users"), set validity period, and preview exactly how Japanese customers will see it — making promotion feel tangible and real.

**Entry Points:**
- Post-publish prompt "Tạo coupon" from Listing Editor (04.1) (primary)
- Business sidebar navigation "Coupon" tab
- Direct URL /business/coupons

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Just published her listing, feeling accomplished, saw the "Create a coupon" prompt and got curious
- **Hope:** "If I offer a small discount, maybe more Japanese customers will actually come in"
- **Worry:** "Is this going to be complicated? Will the Japanese text look right if I can't read it?"

**Main User Goal:** Create a simple, attractive coupon that Japanese customers can understand and use at her restaurant

**Business Goal:** Drive first coupon creation to increase listing engagement and give Lan a measurable conversion tool she can track

**URL/Route:** `/business/coupons`
