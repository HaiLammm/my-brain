# 04.3 Business Dashboard

**Scenario:** 04: Lan Lists Her Business
**Page Number:** 04.3
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** View business analytics — see listing status, coupon status, and learn where to track results over time. This is the command center for Lan's business on DaNangNavi.

**Entry Points:**
- "Đi tới Bảng điều khiển" link from Coupon Manager (04.2) (primary)
- Business sidebar navigation "Bảng điều khiển" tab
- Direct URL /business/dashboard
- Post-login redirect for returning business users

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Completed listing and coupon setup, now arriving at the dashboard for the first time
- **Hope:** "Is this working? Can I see if Japanese customers are finding me?"
- **Worry:** "I set everything up but what if nobody sees it? How will I know?"

**Main User Goal:** Confirm everything is active and understand how to monitor results going forward

**Business Goal:** Deliver a "mission accomplished" feeling that builds confidence, encourage Lan to return and check metrics regularly, reduce churn

**URL/Route:** `/business/dashboard`

---

## Journey Context

This is **page 3 of 3** (final page) in the "Lan Lists Her Business" scenario.
