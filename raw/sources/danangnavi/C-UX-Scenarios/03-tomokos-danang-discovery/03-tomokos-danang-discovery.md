# 03: Tomoko's Da Nang Discovery

**Project:** DaNangNavi
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)
**Design Intent:** Dream Up (D)
**Design Status:** not-started

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Find a great local restaurant, order confidently despite the language barrier, and save money with a coupon — all within her lunch break on vacation.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** #2 Daily User Traffic (50K/day) + #3 User Retention (60%+ returning visitors)
**Objective:** 50,000 unique daily visits, 60% returning visitor rate within 6 months

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Tomoko (Secondary)
**Situation:** 28-year-old Tokyo office worker on day 2 of her 5-day Da Nang vacation. Just finished a morning at My Khe Beach with her friend, hungry for authentic Vietnamese food. TikTok showed beautiful dishes but no addresses, no prices, no way to order.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Find a senpai-verified restaurant nearby with great food, fair prices, and a way to order without embarrassment.

**Worry:** Waste 2 precious vacation hours on a tourist trap where she overpays for mediocre food.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (smartphone)
**Entry:** Friend says "use DaNangNavi, someone on Instagram recommended it." Opens the site for the first time on mobile while walking near My Khe Beach.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Found a senpai-recommended restaurant, used translation tool to order exactly what she wanted, saved ¥500 with a coupon — perfect lunch, posting to Instagram.

**Business Success:**
Tourist session with coupon redemption proving ROI to business owners, Instagram post driving organic traffic to the platform.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Senpai Article/Guide** — Reads "Top 5 Bun Cha near My Khe Beach" by a senpai, picks a restaurant 5 minutes away with 4.8 rating
2. **Translation Tools** — At the restaurant, uses voice translation to order, asks about spice level, confirms the bill
3. **Deals & Coupons** — Redeems 15% off coupon by showing phone screen to cashier, sees savings displayed in JPY ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Tomoko the Tourist (Secondary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** Maximize limited vacation time with efficient discovery of verified, quality experiences
- ❌ **Fear:** Being cheated on prices or led to tourist traps disguised as authentic experiences

**Business Goal:** #2 Daily User Traffic (50K/day) + #3 User Retention (60%+ returning)
