## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Find a trusted restaurant recommendation from senpai content — pick a specific place to eat lunch right now.

**Entry Points:**
- Homepage search: "bun cha near me" → guide result (primary)
- Google search: "ダナン ブンチャー おすすめ" landing page
- Friend shared link via LINE or Instagram
- Homepage Senpai Picks section (01.1)

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Hungry, standing on the street near My Khe Beach, friend said "use DaNangNavi." First time on the platform.
- **Hope:** "I want someone who actually lives here to tell me where to eat — not a generic travel blog."
- **Worry:** "I don't have time to research 50 options. Just give me a good one, close by, where I won't get ripped off."

**Main User Goal:** Pick a specific restaurant within 2 minutes — confirm it's close, fairly priced, and senpai-approved.

**Business Goal:** Convert first-time tourist visitor into platform trust → coupon redemption → Instagram share → returning visitor.

**URL/Route:** `/guides/:slug` (e.g., `/guides/top-5-bun-cha-my-khe`)
