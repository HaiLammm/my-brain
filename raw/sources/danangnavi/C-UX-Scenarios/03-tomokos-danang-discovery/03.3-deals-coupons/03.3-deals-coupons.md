## Journey Context

Tomoko just finished a satisfying meal at a local restaurant near My Khe Beach. She used the translation tools (Page 03.2) to order confidently. While reading the senpai guide earlier (Page 03.1), she noticed a coupon badge. Now feeling happy and adventurous, she opens the Deals page. She finds a dessert coupon for a nearby cafe, redeems it, and sees her trip savings tally. This is the scenario success moment — DaNangNavi has proven its value across discovery, communication, and savings.
