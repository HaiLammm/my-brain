# 01: Naoki's First Week

**Project:** DaNangNavi
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)
**Design Intent:** Dream Up (D)
**Design Status:** not-started

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Find trustworthy guidance to navigate his first week in Da Nang — from finding housing to establishing daily routines, powered by real senpai experiences.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** #1 Thriving Senpai Community — every newcomer who settles successfully becomes a future senpai
**Objective:** 500 active senpai within 6 months

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Naoki (Primary)
**Situation:** 32-year-old Japanese software engineer, just arrived in Da Nang on a work transfer. Day 1, sitting in his hotel room, needs to find an apartment within days, doesn't know which neighborhood suits his office commute, can't read Vietnamese listings or contracts.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Find a senpai-verified step-by-step guide that tells him exactly what to do first, so he doesn't waste time or make costly mistakes.

**Worry:** Sign a bad lease on an overpriced apartment because he can't read Vietnamese contracts or judge fair prices.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (smartphone)
**Entry:** Googles "ダナン 生活ガイド" on his phone while sitting in his hotel room on day 1, clicks a DaNangNavi article ranking on page 1.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Identified the right neighborhood for his commute, found 3 apartment options with fair-price indicators and senpai reviews, completed his first-week checklist — feels "I'm going to be okay here."

**Business Success:**
New high-intent user onboarded into the senpai pipeline, completed first-week checklist (trackable engagement), consumed 5+ pages of content driving platform traffic.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Homepage** — Lands on DaNangNavi, sees welcome banner for newcomers, recognizes this is a platform built for Japanese people in Da Nang
2. **Newcomer Onboarding** — Enters his situation (work transfer, needs apartment), receives a personalized first-week checklist with concrete steps
3. **Area/Neighborhood Guide** — Browses the guide for neighborhoods near his office, reads senpai recommendations with commute times and price ranges
4. **Search/Browse** — Searches for apartments in the recommended neighborhood, filters by budget and Japanese-friendly indicators
5. **Listing Detail** — Views an apartment listing with senpai reviews, fair-price indicator, photos, and bilingual contract guidance — saves to favorites ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Naoki the Newcomer (Primary)

- ✅ **Want:** Find trustworthy step-by-step guidance for first-week essentials from senpai who've been through it
- ❌ **Fear:** Making expensive mistakes on housing, contracts, or services due to language barriers
