# 01.1 Homepage

**Scenario:** 01: Naoki's First Week
**Page Number:** 01.1
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Discover DaNangNavi and recognize it's a platform built specifically for Japanese people in Da Nang — feel welcomed, not lost.

**Entry Points:**
- Google organic search "ダナン 生活ガイド" (primary for Naoki)
- Google organic search "ダナン おすすめ" (primary for Tomoko)
- Direct URL / bookmark (returning users)
- Social media link (Instagram, LINE)

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Just arrived in Da Nang, Googled for help in Japanese, landed here
- **Hope:** "This might actually be the thing I need — a guide made for people like me"
- **Worry:** "Is this another generic travel site, or does it actually understand my situation?"

**Main User Goal:** Understand what DaNangNavi offers and find the path that matches my situation (newcomer vs tourist)

**Business Goal:** Convert first-time visitor into engaged user — route to onboarding (newcomer) or discovery (tourist)

**URL/Route:** `/`

## Design Sections

### Section 1: Hero — Welcome + Search

**Layout:** Split hero with Da Nang background image (Dragon Bridge at sunset or My Khe Beach)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Background | Full-width Da Nang photo, warm color grading, slight overlay |
| Tagline | "ダナンのすべてが、ここに。" (Everything about Da Nang, right here.) |
| Subtitle | "先輩たちの経験で作られた、日本人のためのダナンガイド" (A Da Nang guide for Japanese people, built on senpai experiences) |
| Search bar | Prominent, centered, placeholder: "何をお探しですか？" (What are you looking for?) |
| Quick chips | Below search: レストラン, カフェ, 住まい, ビザ, 病院, 翻訳 |

**Design Rationale:** Search-first matches Naver portal pattern. Japanese tagline confirms instantly "this is for me." Chips reduce cognitive load for overwhelmed newcomers.

---

### Section 2: Newcomer Welcome Banner (CTA)

**Layout:** Full-width coral/orange banner below hero

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Icon | 🎓 or waving hand illustration |
| Headline | "ダナンへようこそ！" (Welcome to Da Nang!) |
| Body | "初めてダナンに来た方へ — 最初の1週間チェックリストで、安心してスタートしましょう" (For first-timers — start your first week with confidence using our checklist) |
| CTA button | "はじめる →" (Get Started) — Coral (#FF6B4A), large touch target |
| Dismiss | Small "×" to close (saves to localStorage) |

**Design Rationale:** This is the PRIMARY conversion path for Naoki. High visual weight (coral on warm white), positioned above the fold on mobile. Disappears for returning users.

**Exit Action:** Clicking "はじめる" → navigates to Newcomer Onboarding (01.2)

### Section 3: Trending / Senpai Picks

**Layout:** Horizontal scroll card row (mobile) / 3-column grid (desktop)

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Section title | "🔥 先輩のおすすめ" (Senpai Picks) |
| Card content | Photo + title + rating + senpai avatar + "先輩認証済み" badge |
| Card types | Mix of restaurants, guides, experiences |
| Card count | 6 cards, 3 visible on desktop |

**Design Rationale:** Social proof — senpai-verified content builds trust immediately. Card-based grid matches Danang Holic reference. Horizontal scroll is familiar LINE/Naver pattern on mobile.

### Section 4: Today's Deals

**Layout:** Compact card row with coral price tags

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Section title | "🎫 今日のお得情報" (Today's Deals) |
| Card content | Business photo + deal description + savings in JPY + expiry countdown |
| Card count | 4 deals |
| CTA | "すべて見る →" (View all) → Deals & Coupons page |

**Design Rationale:** Tomoko's primary hook — deals create immediate tangible value. JPY equivalent pricing addresses tourist markup fear.

### Section 5: Community Highlights

**Layout:** Compact list with avatars

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Section title | "💬 コミュニティ" (Community) |

| CTA | "コミュニティへ →" (Go to Community) |

## Responsive Behavior

| Breakpoint | Changes |
|------------|---------|
| Mobile (< 768px) | Single column, horizontal scroll cards, bottom tab nav, hamburger menu hidden |
| Tablet (768-1024px) | 2-column grid, top nav appears, bottom tab remains |
| Desktop (> 1024px) | 3-column grid, sticky top nav, no bottom tab |
