# 01.4 Search / Browse

**Scenario:** 01: Naoki's First Week
**Page Number:** 01.4
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)

---

## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Search and filter apartment listings in the chosen neighborhood — find 2-3 promising options to evaluate in detail.

**Entry Points:**
- Area Guide "このエリアで物件を探す" CTA (primary — pre-filtered)
- Homepage search bar (keyword search)
- Homepage category quick links
- Bottom tab "検索" (Search)
- Direct URL /search?cat=housing&area=son-tra

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Chose a neighborhood, now needs to find specific apartments
- **Hope:** "Show me apartments that are verified, fairly priced, and have senpai reviews"
- **Worry:** "Will I be able to tell which listings are trustworthy? What's a fair price?"

**Main User Goal:** Find 2-3 apartment options that match my budget, are in the right area, and have positive senpai reviews

**Business Goal:** Drive users to listing detail pages (deeper engagement), demonstrate platform value through verified listings and price transparency

**URL/Route:** `/search`
