## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Evaluate a specific apartment with full details, senpai reviews, fair-price context, and bilingual contract guidance — decide to save or contact.

**Entry Points:**
- Search results card tap (primary)
- Area guide listing link
- Senpai article embedded listing link
- Direct URL /listings/[id]

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Saw a promising listing in search results, tapped to learn more
- **Hope:** "This looks good — I hope the reviews confirm it and the price is fair"
- **Worry:** "What if the photos are misleading? What about the contract — can I trust the landlord?"

**Main User Goal:** Confirm this apartment is a good choice by reading senpai reviews, checking fair price, and understanding what to expect from the contract process

**Business Goal:** Drive user toward save/contact action, demonstrate platform value through trust signals (senpai reviews, price transparency, contract guidance)

**URL/Route:** `/listings/[id]`
