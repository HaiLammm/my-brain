## Overview

**Page Purpose:** Capture Naoki's situation and generate a personalized first-week checklist — make him feel "someone has thought about exactly my problem."

**Entry Points:**
- Homepage welcome banner "はじめる" CTA (primary)
- Direct URL /onboarding (from external guide links)
- Navigation menu "はじめての方" (first-timers)

**User Mental State:**
- **Trigger:** Clicked "Get Started" — committed to trying this platform
- **Hope:** "Finally, a step-by-step plan that tells me exactly what to do"
- **Worry:** "Will this actually be personalized, or just generic tourist tips?"

**Main User Goal:** Get a clear, actionable checklist for my first week based on MY specific situation

**Business Goal:** Capture user context data for personalization, create a "wow" moment that drives retention and account creation

**URL/Route:** `/onboarding`

### Section 3: Generated Checklist

**Layout:** Card list with checkboxes, prioritized by urgency

| Element | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Header | "🎯 あなたの1週間プラン" (Your 1-Week Plan) |
| Subtitle | "先輩たちの経験から、あなたに最適なプランを作りました" (Created from senpai experiences, optimized for you) |

**Checklist Items (example for Naoki's case: work transfer + housing + SIM):**

| Day | Task | Status | Link |
|-----|------|--------|------|
| Day 1 | 📱 SIMカードを買う | ○ | → Guide: SIM card |
| Day 1 | 🍜 近くの日本食レストランを見つける | ○ | → Search: Japanese food |
| Day 2 | 🏠 住むエリアを決める | ○ | → Area Guide (01.3) ★ |
| Day 2-3 | 🏠 物件を内覧する | ○ | → Search: Housing (01.4) |
| Day 3 | 🏦 銀行口座を開設する | ○ | → Guide: Banking |
| Day 4 | 🚗 通勤ルートを確認する | ○ | → Guide: Transport |
| Day 5-7 | 👥 コミュニティに参加する | ○ | → Community Hub |

**Key Design Decisions:**
- Each item links to relevant guide or search page
- ★ marks the recommended next action (highest impact)
- Checkbox state persists (localStorage for guests, account for registered)
- "先輩のアドバイス" expandable tip on each item
