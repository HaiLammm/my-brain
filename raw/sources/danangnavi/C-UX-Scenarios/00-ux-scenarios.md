## Scenarios

### [01: Naoki's First Week](01-naokis-first-week/01-naokis-first-week.md)
**Persona:** Naoki — Trustworthy step-by-step guidance for first-week essentials
**Pages:** Homepage, Newcomer Onboarding, Area/Neighborhood Guide, Search/Browse, Listing Detail
**User Value:** Confident first week — apartment found, daily spots established, no costly mistakes
**Business Value:** New user enters senpai pipeline, 5+ pages consumed, trackable engagement via checklist

---

### [02: Naoki Becomes Senpai](02-naoki-becomes-senpai/02-naoki-becomes-senpai.md)
**Persona:** Naoki — Build social circle with Japanese expats to combat isolation
**Pages:** Community Hub, Community Thread, Events & Meetups, User Profile
**User Value:** Belonging — real friends, local knowledge, identity shift from newcomer to resident
**Business Value:** Active community member generating engagement, future senpai creating content

---

### [03: Tomoko's Da Nang Discovery](03-tomokos-danang-discovery/03-tomokos-danang-discovery.md)
**Persona:** Tomoko — Maximize 5-day vacation with verified, time-efficient discovery
**Pages:** Senpai Article/Guide, Translation Tools, Deals & Coupons
**User Value:** Perfect lunch — senpai-verified restaurant, confident ordering, ¥500 saved with coupon
**Business Value:** Tourist session with coupon redemption proving ROI, Instagram post driving organic traffic

---

### [04: Lan Lists Her Business](04-lan-lists-her-business/04-lan-lists-her-business.md)
**Persona:** Lan — Visibility to Japanese customers without learning Japanese
**Pages:** Listing Editor, Coupon Manager, Business Dashboard
**User Value:** Listing live with translated menu in under 15 minutes, first coupon created
**Business Value:** New business onboarded toward 300 target, coupon ready for trackable redemptions

---

### [05: Naoki & Tomoko Sign Up](05-naoki-tomoko-sign-up/05-naoki-tomoko-sign-up.md)
**Persona:** Naoki / Tomoko — Quick account creation to save content and access community
**Pages:** Sign Up / Login, Notification Center
**User Value:** Signed up in under 30 seconds via LINE, apartment saved, notifications configured
**Business Value:** Registered user with profile data, push notification opt-in, cross-session tracking
