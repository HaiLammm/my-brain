# 02: Naoki Becomes Senpai

**Project:** DaNangNavi
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Method:** Whiteport Design Studio (WDS)
**Design Intent:** Dream Up (D)
**Design Status:** not-started

---

## Transaction (Q1)

**What this scenario covers:**
Join the Japanese expat community, connect with people who understand his situation, and begin the transformation from lonely newcomer to active community member.

---

## Business Goal (Q2)

**Goal:** #4 Community Engagement (10K comments/day) + #1 Thriving Senpai Community
**Objective:** 500 active senpai within 6 months, 10,000 comments/day

---

## User & Situation (Q3)

**Persona:** Naoki (Primary)
**Situation:** 32-year-old Japanese software engineer, been in Da Nang 3 weeks. Apartment secured, daily routine established. But weekends are lonely — colleagues go home to families. He wants Japanese friends who get the expat experience but doesn't know where to find them.

---

## Driving Forces (Q4)

**Hope:** Find a community of Japanese expats who understand his situation and can become real friends.

**Worry:** Spend another Sunday alone scrolling LINE groups from Japan, feeling like moving here was a mistake.

---

## Device & Starting Point (Q5 + Q6)

**Device:** Mobile (smartphone)
**Entry:** Returns to DaNangNavi from a saved bookmark on Sunday afternoon, notices a notification badge about new community activity.

---

## Best Outcome (Q7)

**User Success:**
Posted a question that got helpful replies within hours, signed up for a weekend BBQ meetup — feels "I'm not alone here."

**Business Success:**
Active community member generating engagement (comments, posts), on the path to becoming one of 500 active senpai within months.

---

## Shortest Path (Q8)

1. **Community Hub** — Browses groups and active discussions, sees upcoming events and recent Q&A threads
2. **Community Thread** — Reads a newcomer's question, decides to post his own about weekend activities, gets replies within hours
3. **Events & Meetups** — Discovers a Japanese expat BBQ this Saturday, signs up with one tap
4. **User Profile** — Checks his profile: sees community activity history, senpai progress tracker showing "Newcomer" status ✓

---

## Trigger Map Connections

**Persona:** Naoki the Newcomer (Primary)

**Driving Forces Addressed:**
- ✅ **Want:** Build a social circle with other Japanese expats to combat isolation and create belonging
- ❌ **Fear:** Being isolated and overwhelmed in a city where nobody speaks Japanese

**Business Goal:** #4 Community Engagement (10K comments/day) + #1 Senpai Community (500 active senpai)
