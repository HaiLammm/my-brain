# Business Goals & Objectives

> Strategic goals and measurable objectives for DaNangNavi

**Document:** Trigger Map - Business Goals
**Created:** 2026-04-07
**Status:** COMPLETE

---

## Vision

**DaNangNavi becomes the definitive companion platform for Japanese people in Da Nang — where every newcomer, tourist, and long-term resident finds trustworthy, actionable guidance powered by a thriving senpai community that grows stronger with every person it helps.**

This vision transforms the fragmented landscape of TikTok videos, scattered blog posts, and word-of-mouth into a single, living platform where the Japanese community in Da Nang takes care of its own — and in doing so, bridges cultures and creates lasting value for the entire city.

---

## Business Objectives

### ⭐ PRIMARY GOAL: Build a Thriving Senpai Community (THE ENGINE)

- **Statement:** Create an engaged community of senpai contributors who share real experiences, answer questions, and guide newcomers — making DaNangNavi the trusted source of living knowledge about Da Nang
- **Metric:** Active senpai contributors (users who post/comment weekly)
- **Target:** 500 active senpai within 6 months
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch
- **Impact:** This drives ALL other objectives — senpai create content that attracts newcomers and tourists, which attracts businesses. Without senpai, DaNangNavi is just another listing site

---

### 🚀 PLATFORM ADOPTION (Driven by Senpai Community)

**Objective 1: Daily User Traffic**
- **Statement:** Become the daily-use platform for Japanese people discovering and navigating Da Nang
- **Metric:** Unique daily visits
- **Target:** 50,000/day
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch

**Objective 2: User Retention**
- **Statement:** Deliver enough recurring value that users return regularly, not just for one-time searches
- **Metric:** Returning visitor rate
- **Target:** 60%+ returning visitors
- **Timeline:** 4 months from launch

**Objective 3: Search Visibility**
- **Statement:** Rank as the top result when Japanese people search for Da Nang information
- **Metric:** Google ranking for top 10 Japanese Da Nang keywords
- **Target:** Page 1 for primary keywords (danang おすすめ, danang グルメ, danang 観光, danang 移住)
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch

**Objective 4: Community Engagement**
- **Statement:** Foster an active community where users engage daily through comments, reviews, and discussions
- **Metric:** Daily community comments
- **Target:** 10,000 comments/day
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch

---

### 🌟 BUSINESS VALUE (Real-World Benefits for Da Nang's Economy)

**Note:** These are opportunities DaNangNavi creates FOR Vietnamese business owners and the local economy — connecting Japanese spending power with Da Nang businesses that deserve it.

**Objective 5: Business Onboarding**
- **Statement:** Onboard local businesses who want to reach Japanese customers through verified listings
- **Metric:** Registered businesses with active listings
- **Target:** 300 businesses
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch
- **Benefit to Business Owners:** Direct access to Japanese customers without needing Japanese language skills or cultural knowledge

**Objective 6: Business Owner Satisfaction**
- **Statement:** Ensure businesses see real value — more foot traffic, bookings, and revenue from Japanese customers
- **Metric:** Business owner satisfaction score
- **Target:** 70%+ satisfaction rate
- **Timeline:** 6 months from launch
- **Benefit to Business Owners:** Measurable ROI through booking analytics and coupon redemption tracking

**Objective 7: Revenue Generation**
- **Statement:** Build sustainable revenue through promoted listings, booking commissions, and coupon partnerships
- **Metric:** Monthly revenue from B2B services
- **Target:** Positive unit economics by month 6
- **Timeline:** Revenue model active by month 4
- **Benefit to Business Owners:** Pay only for results — performance-based pricing aligned with their success
