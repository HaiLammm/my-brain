## Layer 2: Project Context (Cumulative)

### Initial: Product Brief
**Business:** DaNangNavi — companion app for Japanese people in Da Nang
**Model:** B2B2C — free for Japanese users, revenue from Vietnamese businesses
**Challenge:** Fragmented information landscape (TikTok, blogs, word-of-mouth) — none actionable
**Competitive Moat:** Senpai community network effect — flywheel can't be replicated
**Constraints:** Solo developer, 7-day deadline, ~10 demo screens, Next.js + mock data

### User Archetypes (from Brief)
1. **Tanaka-san (New Expat)** — needs first-week guidance, housing, food, transport
2. **Yamada-san (Tourist)** — wants quick discovery, deals, language support
3. **Suzuki-san (Retiree)** — long-term needs, medical, visa (Phase 2)
4. **Vietnamese business owners** — want to reach Japanese customers

### Brand & Design Context
- Tone: Warm senpai, casual but trustworthy
- Visual: Navy + Coral + Teal, Flat + Glassmorphism, Noto Sans JP
- Languages: EN (source) → JP (primary UI) → VN (secondary)
- Tech: Next.js App Router, Tailwind, i18n, Server Components

### Added: Business Goals
- Vision: Definitive companion platform powered by senpai community
- PRIMARY: 500 active senpai (THE ENGINE)
- SECONDARY: 50K daily visits, 60% retention, 10K comments, Page 1 SEO
- TERTIARY: 300 businesses, 70% satisfaction, sustainable revenue
- Flywheel: Newcomers → senpai → content → users → businesses → revenue

### Added: Target Groups
- PRIMARY: Naoki the Newcomer (Japanese expat, future senpai)
- SECONDARY: Tomoko the Tourist (high-volume demand, proves B2B value)
- TERTIARY: Lan the Listing Owner (Vietnamese business, revenue model)
- Rationale: Focused on 3 groups to avoid dilution

### Added: Driving Forces
- Per persona: 3 positive + 3 negative drivers
- All context-specific (usage goals, not life goals)
- All include DaNangNavi Promise/Answer
- Cross-group patterns: language barrier #1, trust deficit #2

### Added: Prioritization
- Strategic focus: Naoki's first-week experience → senpai conversion
- Feature Impact: Language tools + trust signals are foundation, not features
- Design priority: Naoki > Tomoko > Lan

## Layer 3: Domain Research

### Step: Business Goals & Target Groups

**Research Conducted:**
1. WebSearch: "Japanese tourists Da Nang Vietnam behavior preferences pain points 2025 2026"
   - Finding: Japan→Vietnam tourism +38% in 2025-2026; 814K Japanese visitors in 2025
   - Finding: Workation and longer stays reshaping demand; Da Nang benefits from lifestyle hospitality
   - Relevance: Validates large and growing target market for both tourist and expat personas

2. WebSearch: "Japanese expats Vietnam community challenges daily life problems"
   - Finding: Language barrier is #1 challenge; social isolation is major risk
   - Finding: Bureaucratic complexity (visa, TRC, work permits) surprises newcomers
   - Finding: Community building is essential for mental/emotional wellbeing
   - Relevance: Directly informs Naoki's driving forces — isolation and language barriers

3. WebSearch: "local business guide platform success metrics community platform KPIs"
   - Finding: DAU/MAU, engagement rate, retention, NPS are standard community KPIs
   - Finding: Content interaction (comments, shares, reactions) measures community health
   - Finding: Build KPIs directly off business goals
   - Relevance: Validates our metrics structure (comments/day, returning visitors)

### Step: Driving Forces & Prioritization

4. WebSearch: "Vietnamese business owners marketing to Japanese tourists challenges SMB digital marketing"
   - Finding: Vietnamese SMBs lack IT knowledge and digital marketing expertise
   - Finding: >50% SMBs spend <1 hour/day on marketing
   - Finding: Localization requires deep cultural understanding beyond translation
   - Relevance: Validates Lan's fear of complexity and need for simple, Vietnamese-language tools

5. WebSearch: "Japanese retirees Da Nang Vietnam long stay community needs medical services"
   - Finding: Da Nang has Japanese Consulate General — reassuring for expats
   - Finding: Quality private healthcare available (Family Medical Practice, Vinmec, Hoan My)
   - Finding: 45-day visa-free stay for Japanese (changed from 15 days in 2023)
   - Relevance: Validates Da Nang as viable long-term destination; supports future retiree persona

**Key Insights:**
- Japanese tourism to Vietnam is booming — timing is right for DaNangNavi
- Language barrier is universally the #1 pain point — must be solved at platform level
- Vietnamese SMBs need extremely simple tools — complex dashboards will fail
- Da Nang has infrastructure (consulate, hospitals) that supports long-term Japanese community
