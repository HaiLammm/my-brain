# Product Brief: DaNangNavi

## Strategic Summary

DaNangNavi is the definitive companion app for Japanese people in Da Nang — the one place that has everything you need, whether you're planning your first trip or have lived here for years. Built on real experiences shared by senpai (trusted long-term residents), always up-to-date local knowledge, and a judgement-free space where you can search for anything — even things you'd never ask anyone about.

Currently, Japanese people rely on TikTok (inspiring but not actionable), Danang Holic (quality editorial but one-way), or word-of-mouth (high trust but doesn't scale). DaNangNavi bridges the gap by creating a community-first cultural bridge — turning inspiration into action, and turning tourists into people who love Da Nang and come back.

The competitive moat is a network effect powered by the senpai community: more senpai sharing creates better content, which attracts more newcomers, who eventually become senpai themselves. This flywheel cannot be easily replicated by editorial-first competitors.

## Product Concept

### Core Structural Idea

**"Community-first cultural bridge"** — DaNangNavi organizes everything around the Japanese community and cultural understanding of Da Nang, not around listings/search.

### Implementation Layers

| Layer | Role |
|-------|------|
| Community (foundation) | Senpai sharing, groups, discussions — creates belonging & trust |
| Cultural Discovery (content) | Articles, guides, local experiences — creates deep impressions |
| Communication Bridge (tools) | Translation, voice assist, phrasebook — removes action barriers |
| Listing & Services (utility) | Find places, booking, coupons — serves specific needs |

### Rationale

Coupons and listings create short-term value (one-time use). Community + cultural understanding creates an emotional bond with Da Nang — turning tourists into return visitors, and return visitors into expats. This is the natural growth loop.

## Business Model

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Model | B2B2C (Two-sided platform) |
| End Users (B2C) | Japanese people — free forever |
| Paying Customers (B2B) | Vietnamese business owners — ads, booking commission, coupons |
| Phase 1 (Launch) | Completely free for both sides — build user base & content |
| Phase 2 (Monetize) | Ads, promoted listings, booking fees, coupon commissions |
| Implication | Two separate experiences needed: app for end users + dashboard for business owners |

### Business Customer Profile

| Aspect | Detail |
|--------|--------|
| Priority Industry | Tourism (tour operators, hotels, homestays, experiences) |
| Expansion | Restaurants, salons, visa services, medical... |
| Company Size | SMB — small/medium businesses in Da Nang |
| Decision Maker | Business owner directly (no intermediaries) |
| Buying Process | Fast, single decision-maker, needs to see demo first |
| Primary Need | Reach Japanese customers, increase bookings, visibility |
| Barriers | Language (Vietnamese only), needs simple UX, needs immediate value |

## Competitive Landscape

| Alternative | Strength | Weakness | DaNangNavi Wins By |
|-------------|----------|----------|-------------------|
| TikTok | Viral content, easy access | Not actionable, not location-aware | Turning inspiration into action |
| Danang Holic | Quality editorial, Japanese UX, LINE bot | One-way, no community, no real-time | Two-way community, senpai system, life admin |
| Vietnam Sketch | Deep expat lifestyle content | Traditional webmagazine, not interactive | Interactive platform, communication tools |
| Asking friends | High trust, personalized | Depends on luck, doesn't scale | Senpai community = "asking friends" at scale |
| Do nothing | Zero effort | Price gouging, isolation, missed experiences | Immediate risk reduction, better experiences |

### Unfair Advantage

Community-powered platform with network effect — Danang Holic can add features, but cannot create an engaged senpai community overnight. DaNangNavi builds a flywheel: more senpai = better content = more newcomers = more future senpai.

## Tone of Voice

### Attributes

1. **Warm & Welcoming** — Newcomers need to feel received, not abandoned
2. **Trustworthy & Reliable** — Japanese users value accuracy and credibility
3. **Friendly & Casual** — Like senpai talking to kouhai, not customer service
4. **Reassuring & Supportive** — Reduce anxiety for people in a foreign place

### Microcopy Examples

| Context | DaNangNavi Tone |
|---------|----------------|
| Welcome | "Welcome to Da Nang! We're here for you" |
| Search | "What are you looking for?" |
| Empty state | "Nothing found yet — try a different keyword?" |
| Error | "Something went wrong — let's try again" |
| Loading | "Finding that for you..." |
| First visit | "Let's start exploring!" |
| Coupon | "Grab this deal" |
| Review | "Share your experience" |

### Do's and Don'ts

| Do | Don't |
|----|-------|
| Speak like a friend — casual, warm | Overly formal corporate tone |
| Use moderate emoji where appropriate | Spam emoji or be overly cutesy |
| Reassure when user might be anxious | Judge or compromise privacy |
| Keep it short and clear | Be verbose or complex |
